eSIM vs Roaming in Southeast Asia: What Actually Saves You Money

Written by the Mimajo team · Published this week · 5 min read

Roaming plans look convenient until you check the daily cap. Across Thailand, Vietnam, Japan and South Korea, a regional eSIM data pack came out cheaper in every case except very short trips of two days or less.

What we compared

Telco day-pass roaming versus a regional eSIM data pack, same data allowance, same four-country route.

Where roaming still wins

Trips of a day or two — the eSIM's activation overhead isn't worth it for that short a window, and a single day-pass is simple enough to just buy and forget.

Where eSIM pulls ahead

Anything over three days. The per-day cost drops fast on multi-day eSIM bundles, and you avoid the "forgot to turn off roaming" bill shock entirely.

The one thing to check first

Confirm your phone actually supports eSIM before you fly — most phones from the last four years do, but it's a five-minute check worth doing at home, not at the gate.
