Canggu villa prices have roughly doubled in five years. These towns still feel like the Bali people were chasing a decade ago.

## Sidemen
Rice-terrace views without the Ubud crowds, and villa rates a fraction of Ubud's.

## Amed
A quiet black-sand coastline on the east side, popular with divers, largely skipped by everyone else.

## Munduk
Cool mountain air, waterfalls within walking distance, and some of the best-value guesthouses on the island.

## Lovina
Dolphin-watching without Nusa Penida's day-tripper crowds, and seafood dinners on the beach for a few dollars.

## Sanur
Calmer than Seminyak, walkable, and still close enough to the airport for an easy last night before a flight home.
