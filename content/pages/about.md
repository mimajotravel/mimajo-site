Mimajo started with a simple frustration: travel deal sites either feel decades out of date, or they're really just ad networks wearing a "deals" costume. We wanted something in between — a site that actually checks its own deals, written by people, refreshed regularly, and built to feel like it belongs in 2026 rather than 2010.

## Where the name comes from

Mimajo is three syllables, three colours: **Mi** (rose pink), **Ma** (coral peach), and **Jo** (deep teal). Two feminine, one masculine — a small detail, but it's baked into everything from the logo to how we think about who we're building this for: everyone who wants a good trip without overpaying for it.

## How we check a deal

Every deal on Mimajo is reviewed before it's published — real prices, real availability, checked against what you'd pay booking directly. We link out to the merchant or travel partner to complete your booking; Mimajo doesn't process payments for deals itself. Some of those links are affiliate links, which is covered in full on our [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure.html) page.

## Who's behind it

Mimajo is an independent, Asia-based travel publisher, currently run as a small team. We're not owned by an airline, hotel group, or booking platform — our only job is finding deals worth your time.
