Mimajo started with a simple frustration: while the internet is flooded with online travel deals, finding ones actually worth booking is exhausting. Most travel deal sites either feel decades out of date or turn out to be ad networks wearing a "deals" costume. We built Mimajo to change that. Each week, we scout online travel platforms across Asia to identify, verify, and share only the ***absolute best travel offers*** and ***insider discounts***. Curated by real travelers, refreshed continuously, and built for 2026 and beyond, Mimajo cuts through the noise so you can plan smarter and choose your next Asian adventure with complete confidence.

## Where the name comes from

It starts with ***"Mima" (密码)***, the Chinese word for *password*. That’s exactly what we aim to be: your **master key to unlocking the secret, unadvertised deals** and hidden travel savings across Asia that most people miss. We track down these insider offers and decode them daily for our growing traveler community. Beyond the name, Mimajo comes to life through three distinct syllables and shades: Mi (rose pink), Ma (coral peach), and Jo (deep teal). It’s a design identity as vibrant as our mission: helping everyone travel better, explore further, and never pay full price.

## How we check a deal

Every deal on Mimajo is reviewed before it's published: real prices, real availability, checked against what you'd pay booking directly. We link out to the merchant or travel partner to complete your booking. Mimajo currently does not process payments for deals itself. Some of those links are affiliate links, which is covered in full on our [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure.html) page.

## Who's behind it

Mimajo is an independent, Asia-based travel publisher, currently run as a small team. We're not owned by an airline, hotel group, or booking platform, our only job is finding deals worth your time.
