*At **Mimajo** ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal data is handled securely and transparently. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit [mimajo.co](https://mimajo.co) (the "Site"), subscribe to our travel deal alerts, or join our membership platform.*

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect personal data that you voluntarily provide to us, as well as data gathered automatically when you interact with our Site:

* **Account & Subscription Data:**

   Name, email address, password, and travel preferences (e.g., home airport, favorite travel destinations, deal frequency preferences) when you subscribe to deal alerts, join our membership, or contact us.
* **Payment & Transactional Data:**

   If you purchase a paid membership tier, payment details are processed directly by our third-party payment gateways (e.g., Stripe). We do not store full credit card details on our servers.
* **Technical & Usage Data:**

   IP address, device type, browser model, operating system, pages viewed, time spent on pages, and referring URLs collected via cookies and server logs.
* **Affiliate Interaction Data:**

   Information on whether you clicked an outbound affiliate deal link, enabling us and our travel partners to verify deal completions and commissions.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data for the following core business purposes:

* To send you curated travel deal alerts, flash deals, and newsletters matching your subscription choices.
* To manage and maintain your Mimajo account or membership benefits.
* To respond to customer support inquiries and process feedback.
* To analyze Site usage, improve user experience, and optimize travel deal algorithms.
* To comply with legal, regulatory, and audit obligations under applicable laws, including Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

## 3. Sharing & Disclosure of Data

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. However, we may share your data with trusted partners under strict confidentiality agreements:

* **Service Providers:**

   Cloud hosting platforms, email distribution systems, analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics), and payment processors that help operate our Site.
* **Affiliate Networks & Travel Partners:**

   When you click a deal link on Mimajo to book flights, hotels, or experiences with a partner (e.g., airlines, OTA platforms, booking portals), cookie identifiers or anonymous click IDs may be passed to attribute the booking to Mimajo.
* **Legal Requirements:**

   If required by court order, law enforcement request, or applicable law to protect our rights, users, or safety.

## 4. International Data Transfers

Your personal data may be transferred to, stored, and processed outside your country of residence (including in Singapore, the United States, or other regions where our cloud servers and service providers operate). We ensure that any international transfers comply with applicable data protection laws, including applying standard contractual clauses or equivalent legal safeguards under the PDPA.

## 5. Cookies and Affiliate Tracking

We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience and support our affiliate business model:

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact certain site functions or membership log-in capabilities.

## 6. Data Security & Retention

We implement appropriate technical, administrative, and physical security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law (e.g., tax or accounting requirements).

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

* **Access & Correction:**

   Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you or request corrections to inaccurate data.
* **Withdrawal of Consent / Deletion:**

   Withdraw consent to receive marketing emails or request the deletion of your account at any time. Every travel alert email includes a 1-click "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom.
* **Data Portability:**

   Request the transfer of your data to another provider where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

## 8. Third-Party Links

Mimajo features curated links to external travel merchant websites, booking engines, and airline pages. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any site you visit.

## 9. Children's privacy

Mimajo is intended for a general audience and is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our legal obligations, technology, or business practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the latest revisions took effect.

## 11. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via our [contact page](/contact.html).
