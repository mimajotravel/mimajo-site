7 Things We Always Do Before a Flight to Save Money

Written by the Mimajo team · Published this week · 4 min read

None of these are secret tricks — just a checklist we run every time, in order.

1. Search in an incognito window

Clears any price-tracking cookies that can nudge fares upward on repeat searches.

2. Compare Tuesday departures

Still, on average, the cheapest day to fly across most Southeast Asian routes.

3. Check the airline's own site last

Sometimes cheaper than the OTA once seat sales are on, sometimes not — worth the extra tab.

4. Set a price alert instead of booking on sight

Fares fluctuate daily; a week of watching almost always beats booking on the first search.

5. Price the "flexible dates" view

Shifting by even a day or two either side of your target date can save significantly.

6. Pack carry-on only when the route allows it

Skips the checked-bag fee entirely on budget carriers, where it can cost more than the seat.

7. Book the connecting flight separately when it's cheaper

Occasionally two one-way fares beat a single connecting itinerary — always worth a quick check.
