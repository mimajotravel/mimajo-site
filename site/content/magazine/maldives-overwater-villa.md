Overwater villas usually start at $1,000+ a night. We paid $689 for three nights — here's what actually moved the price.

## Shoulder season is the real lever

Late April to early June sits between peak season and monsoon. Rates on the same villa can drop 40–50% versus December, and the weather is still mostly clear.

## Email the resort directly

Once we found our shortlist on a booking site, we emailed each resort directly asking for their best rate with breakfast included. Two of four replied with a lower price than any booking platform showed.

## Skip the resort-island premium

A villa on a smaller, independently run island cost roughly 60% of an equivalent room on a big resort-brand island, with the same overwater setup and house-reef snorkelling.
