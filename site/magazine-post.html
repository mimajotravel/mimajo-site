48 Hours in Bangkok: Where to Eat, Stay and Skip the Tourist Traps

Written by the Mimajo team · Published this week · 6 min read

Bangkok rewards travellers who skip the obvious and wander one street over. This is the loop we'd walk if we only had two days and wanted every meal to be worth it.

Day 1: Chinatown to Ari

Start at the Yaowarat food stalls before 6pm, while the queues are still short. Head north to Ari in the evening — it's quieter, full of independent coffee shops, and a fraction of the price of Sukhumvit.

The best meal we had cost under $3 and came from a stall with no sign, just a queue of regulars.

Where to stay

Pick a hotel near the BTS Ari or Phrom Phong stations — both put you within a 20-minute ride of everywhere on this list, without paying Sukhumvit prices for the room itself.

Day 2: Old Town by boat

Take the Chao Phraya express boat instead of a taxi — it's cheaper, faster in traffic, and gives you a proper look at the city from the water on the way to the Grand Palace and Wat Arun.
