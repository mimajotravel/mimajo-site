The Carry-On-Only Packing List We Use for Every Trip Under 10 Days

Written by the Mimajo team · Published this week · 3 min read

The same packing list, trip after trip. Nothing exotic — just consistent enough that packing takes ten minutes.

The system

Everything rolls, not folds. One packing cube for tops, one for bottoms, one for anything that could leak.

The one splurge worth it

A compression cube for worn laundry — keeps the rest of the bag from smelling by day four.

What we leave behind on purpose

A second pair of "just in case" shoes. In five years of carry-on-only travel, we've never once needed it.
