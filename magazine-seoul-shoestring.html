Seoul on a Shoestring: A 3-Day Neighbourhood-by-Neighbourhood Plan

Written by the Mimajo team · Published this week · 7 min read

Seoul is easy to overspend in if you bounce across the city. Base each day in one neighbourhood instead.

Day 1: Hongdae

Street food, indie cafes, and the cheapest late-night eats in the city. A full day of wandering costs less than one sit-down meal in Gangnam.

Day 2: Ikseon-dong & Bukchon

Hanok village streets, small tea houses, and free entry days at several nearby palaces if you time it right.

Day 3: Mangwon Market

A local wet market turned street-food strip that most tourist itineraries skip entirely — and where we ate the best meal of the trip for under $5.
